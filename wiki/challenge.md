# The UBS challenge

## What UBS asks for

Build a "financial twin": a model you can change, to see what would happen. UBS's slogan: "Don't predict the future. Make it explorable."

We must show:
- one made-up person or company
- one change that works in the demo
- a before and after comparison
- the guesses behind every number, open for everyone to see

Extra points for a chat assistant, and for showing how sure the numbers are.

UBS warns that 3D alone does not make a twin. The maths behind it must be strong.

## How judges score

- **Money maths (25 points):** cost, yearly savings, years until it pays back.
- **What-if changes (20):** today vs. after the upgrade, plus "what if energy gets more expensive?"
- **Proof it works (15):** one result checked by hand, matching the app.
- **Easy to understand (15):** a non-expert sees why the numbers change.
- **New ideas (15):** energy, comfort and money together.
- **Honesty (10):** every number marked as a fact, a guess or a result.

Judges will ask: Which guesses matter most? How did you check the maths? What happens if someone enters something silly?

## What most teams will do

A student called Alexia, three lines on a chart, a chatbot, and "this is not financial advice" at the bottom. Last year a dashboard with a chatbot won. A 3D model has won a LauzHack prize before, so 3D alone is not new.

## How we stand out

1. **Open with this building's energy bill.** "It tells you what you paid. It doesn't tell you how to pay less." This follows UBS's own idea: a statement only looks back.
2. **Two buildings side by side:** today and after the upgrade.
3. **Colour every number** by type: fact, guess or result.
4. **One clear limit:** "This heat pump pays off as long as electricity costs less than X."
5. **Use UBS's own test case:** "energy bills double". It is in their scenario list.
6. **Let a judge try to break it.** The app says clearly what went wrong.

Don't say "predict". Don't make a chatbot the main thing. Don't depend on the Wi-Fi.

## Practical facts

- Coding stops Sunday 12:00. Judging is 12:30–15:00. Prizes are 15:00–17:00.
- We can reuse old Enoptim work, but we must say what we built this weekend.
- No real customer data in the demo.
- **No slides.** The organisers confirmed this on 2026-11-21.
- **Strictly 30 seconds at most to connect to the big screen.** There's no extra time. If it isn't working at 30 seconds, we present from the laptop screen.
- About 245 participants plus about 55 staff and challenge hosts, so about 300 people in total (organisers, 2026-11-21). This matches the 300 people in `data/events.csv`.
- Pitch length: 3 minutes on the main stage, 5 minutes for UBS (see D6).
