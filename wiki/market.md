# Market

## Why now

- Buildings use almost 40% of Switzerland's energy, and cause about a quarter of its CO₂ ([Swiss Federal Office of Energy](https://www.admin.ch/de/newnsb/qGTPQ1ZqqKag)).
- By 2030, EU law will force owners to upgrade the worst 16% of office and other non-home buildings ([EU](https://energy.ec.europa.eu/topics/energy-efficiency/energy-performance-buildings/energy-performance-buildings-directive_en)).
- Switzerland gave out CHF 636 million for building upgrades in 2025 ([Swiss Federal Office of Energy](https://www.admin.ch/de/newnsb/qGTPQ1ZqqKag)).

## Why UBS cares

- **UBS lends money for green buildings.** The building must have a top energy rating (A or B) and no oil or gas heating ([UBS](https://ubs.com/ch/en/corporates/real-estate/manage/loan-green.html)). An upgrade in our twin could make a building qualify. The only stated benefit is a one-time bonus of CHF 2,000, with no lower interest rate listed. It is for buildings that companies own to rent out or use, not public buildings. (Checked 2026-11-21.)
- **UBS owns buildings through its property funds.** It has written climate targets for 2050 into the funds' rules. One fund (Foncipars) now uses AI to run its buildings better, after a pilot ([UBS](https://www.ubs.com/ch/en/assetmanagement/capabilities/real-estate/sustainability.html)).
- **UBS and the property manager Wincasa offer upgrade advice with financing help:** energy analysis, a renovation plan, and UBS advice on loans (2024) ([Wincasa](https://www.wincasa.ch/en-ch/uber-wincasa/news/article/ubs-und-wincasa-lancieren-neue-beratungsloesung-fuer-energetische-sanierungen-von-renditeliegenschaften)).

In short: every upgrade in our twin is something UBS could lend money for.

## What already exists

- **Expert software** (EnergyPlus, IES): one study takes weeks, and only consultants can use it.
- **Energy dashboards** (Siemens, Aedifion, Spacewell): show what happened, not what could happen.
- **Online calculators:** use national averages, not your building.

The gap: no tool lets a normal person test an upgrade on their own building and get an answer the finance director trusts.

## Where Enoptim fits

Today Enoptim finds wasted energy in a building's real meter readings. The twin is the next step: from "what you waste today" to "what each upgrade would save".
