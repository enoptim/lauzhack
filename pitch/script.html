Pitch script

One line is one breath. Pause between lines. "…" means a short pause. Blue words get the stress. Grey lines are what you do, not what you say.

0:00–0:20

1. The hook

Walk on. Hold up the bill. Wait two seconds.

This is an energy bill.

Point up and around the room.

It's last year's bill… for this building.

The one we're all sitting in right now.

Two hundred and twenty thousand francs.

Pause.

And it tells us one thing:

what we paid.

It doesn't tell us how to pay less.

Put the bill down. Turn to the screen.

So we built a copy of this building.

One where we can try things out…

before we pay for them.

0:20–0:35

2. Who we are

We call it a digital twin.

A living copy of a real building.

Look at the UBS judges.

You asked us for a twin of a person.

We picked something with much bigger bills.

Short pause for the laugh.

But the people who run a building have the same problem as you and me…

big bills… big decisions…

and no way to test them first.

The four of us built it… this weekend.

Point at each person as you say the name.

Casper built the 3D model.

Oscar did the simulation maths.

Theodor did the data… and the search for the best plan.

And I'm Linus. I built a lot of the app…

and I do the talking.
