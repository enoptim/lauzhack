# The What-If Building

## Short description

Try upgrades in a 3D twin of EPFL’s BC building and see how they could change its energy bill, indoor air and budget before spending a franc.

## Long description

An energy bill tells you what you paid, not what to change. In The What-If Building, you can explore a 3D copy of the building hosting LauzHack, pick a floor and try an upgrade such as smarter lighting or better ventilation. Compare the upfront cost, energy use, indoor air and how long the upgrade could take to pay for itself against keeping the current setup. The twin runs each plan through 200 possible futures, so you can see what happens if electricity prices jump or old equipment fails early. An AI assistant can help you find a plan that fits your budget. The building is real; the equipment, occupancy and costs are model assumptions, not measurements.
